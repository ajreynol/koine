# The documentation

One document per question, so that a question has one obvious place to be
answered. There is no second overview here: [`../README.md`](../README.md) is the front page and
the whole of what anything below may assume has been read.

| document | its job |
| --- | --- |
| [`coherence.md`](coherence.md) | **the maintenance entry point**: what koine is responsible for, the division between what an agent decides alone and what is asked first, and the open work. Not linked from the front page, by the same convention that keeps it out of a reader's way |
| [`maintaining.md`](maintaining.md) | **how a protocol is maintained here**: the ground-truth principle that makes a copy safe, koine's reading of anoieu's practice, unverified by them, with the incident behind each rule, and the three stages a protocol moves through on its way to being held |
| [`postmortem-protocol.md`](postmortem-protocol.md) | **the postmortem protocol**: a uniform record of what happened to a repository, who was involved, how it came out and what was learned — its fields, its two levels, and what koine's tooling does with one |
| [`drift.md`](drift.md) | **the prompt-drift check**: what it compares, what it asks of a script, how a customer fetches and calls it, and the two specs that reproduce the copies it replaces |
| [`discussion.md`](discussion.md) | **the standing channel to the rest of the ecosystem**, and the intake for everything this repository builds: anoieu and dokimasia decide what koine does, and this is where an ask arrives. Anything that is not a defect report — a request, a proposal, a question about somebody's intent, a notice that something here is moving under them. It opens with the rule that no agent acts on it unbidden |

A document not worth a row here is not worth adding.
